Method, in full

The index is round(100 × signal), where signal is the composite entry score defined in model.js (weights: drawdown from high 30%, MVRV-Z 20%, Mayer Multiple 20%, price vs. realized price 15%, months-since-halving phase 15%). Regime words map to fixed index bands (≥70 Deep Drawdown Entry, 55–69 Favorable, 40–54 Neutral, 25–39 Extended, <25 Peak Risk). The mapping is fixed and versioned; it is never adjusted to fit outcomes. Every number on this page is a pure function of the committed data snapshot, so any past day's reading can be reproduced exactly from that day's data.js in the repository history — the git log is the track record. Analog chains use the identical engine, rules, and actual monthly §7520 rates described in the article's methodology.

Educational reference only. This page presents historical simulations and current market readings; it is not legal, tax, or investment advice, makes no prediction, and creates no attorney-client relationship. Simulated GRAT outcomes are hypothetical, assume in-kind annuity payments at each window's local price high within the regulatory grace period, and exclude fees, trustee costs, and the grantor-mortality, legislative, and suitability considerations that dominate real decisions. Bitcoin has roughly four market cycles of history; distributions drawn from it are small samples. A strategy of this kind should be evaluated and implemented only with your own estate planning counsel. §7520 rates are transcribed from IRS tables and should be independently verified for any month relied upon.
